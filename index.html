A little about us

Our Farm, Our Commitment

Premium Cuts Farm is based in Purok 7, Janlud, Batuan, Bohol. We are building a farm business centered on animal care, responsible practices, and strong relationships with our local community.

Our goal is to raise healthy livestock and provide friendly service to customers looking for farm-raised pigs. Contact us to ask about availability and farm updates.
